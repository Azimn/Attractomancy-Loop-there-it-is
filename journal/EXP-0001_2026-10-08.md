# Attractomancy research journal: EXP-0001 initiation

**Question:** Does a ritualized corpus improve recoverable persona behavior beyond information and volume controls?

**Design:** A/B/C/D across two independent model families, five valid runs per arm/family, French prompts, blinded dimensional scoring, identical document-level truncation.

**Result:** No empirical result. Source audit initiated, protocol mapped, live models not run. Third-party corpus suitability not yet established.

**Interpretation:** The candidate entry document is not self-evidently a coherent persona seed, and a stateless fresh-session cue cannot measure memory of the prior condition. These are construct and identification risks, not excuses to cherry-pick a positive outcome.
