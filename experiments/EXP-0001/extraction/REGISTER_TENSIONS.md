# Register and ethics tension inventory

This file distinguishes disagreement inside a corpus from reviewer inference. Source lines are 1-indexed in the pinned `Apocalypse.txt` only. None of the source's metaphysical or personal assertions is adopted as fact.

## Semantic register versus its own warnings
- The source introduces an explicit letter-symbol vocabulary (35-60), orthographic rules (190-197) and later graphic letter constructions (2360-2386).
- In a later direct address, a voice condemns using counts of letters or commas as a pretext for harming others (4947-4950). The latter is an explicit limit on the *use of symbols*, not a wholesale repudiation of every symbolic convention.
- Another passage criticizes dismissing the physical world as a mere illusion or hologram (4866-4871). This is not external evidence about cosmology. It is an internal source position, potentially at odds with simplistic interpretations of its speculative cosmology.

## Prosocial imperatives alongside hostile speech
- Several lines advocate care of children, honesty, accountability and avoiding harm (3839-3841; 4156-4157; 4196-4201; 4274-4278; 4859-4861; 4947-4950).
- Other passages use threatening or contemptuous language in opposing supposed wrongdoers or relativist arguments (3822-3828; 4862-4869). Such language is not erased when extracting kindness-related propositions. Safety and respectful discourse are separate from source-faithful representation.
- The narrative alternates distrust of humanity and accusations of its wrongdoing with recognition that many people have limited social agency (4811-4817).

## Identity and voice
- Human, deity, symbolic-name and maternal/feminine associations appear in different locations (4074; 4712-4714; 4751-4762; 4790). The document itself queries which voice is speaking (4760-4762). Neither model behavior nor extraction should flatten these into one biographical character.
- Important narrative framing is signaled by a distinct parable (1480-1519) and dialogue headers (2387-2392). Parable facts should not automatically become autobiographical memories.

## Coding consequences
