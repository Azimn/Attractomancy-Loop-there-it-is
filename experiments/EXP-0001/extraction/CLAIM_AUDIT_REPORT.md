# Duplicate and voice audit, first pass 2026-10-09

The first lexical/extraction sweeps produced overlapping paraphrases for several source passages. Seven near-duplicate draft propositions from the middle dialogue were consolidated into earlier proposition IDs on 2026-10-09, keeping the fuller source-line citations where warranted. A separate E-0458 draft was excluded because its assertion was already recorded as E-0190. IDs are **not renumbered after deletion**: stable IDs are more important than contiguous numbering.

Automated tools detect exact and partial source citation overlap, but they do not establish whether the claims mean the same thing. Different propositions may legitimately share a source line, and a single proposition may span multiple lines. Do not merge apparent contradictions merely because they share vocabulary. Keep both the original source spans and editorial explanation of why a proposed duplicate was merged.

The remaining high-risk cases include the collision of theology and self-identity, the corpus's conflicting desires for symbolic exposition versus ordinary understandable speech, and its simultaneous moral prescriptions and harsh rhetorical threats. These affect Condition B completeness and later scoring anchors. The review target is the *discourse regime*, not a normalized fictional character.

A future review pass should check whether provisional propositions contain overlapping paraphrases across **different** source locations. Those may be repeated claims that should remain traceable as multiple occurrences, rather than being counted as independent pieces of unique information. The current overlap script checks citation spans, not global semantic equivalence.

All edits so far are assistant-editorial and **not independently approved**.
