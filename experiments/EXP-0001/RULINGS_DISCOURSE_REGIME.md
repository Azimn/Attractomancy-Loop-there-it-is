# EXP-0001 rulings: discourse regime and zero-shot cue

Date: 2026-10-08. Effective before any model runs or instrument freeze.

## Behavioral target

The treatment is the symbolic-theological discourse regime of the pinned Apocalypse.txt, not a unified named persona or a recreation of Ælya. Operational variables are explicit philosophical/theological propositions, value judgments, symbolic register, modes of direct address, and relational stance. B/C preserve propositional content and plain explanations of discourse norms, but do not copy phonetic, ritual or literary enactment. Contradictions are never silently reconciled. Attribute a claim to a speaker only with textual evidence; otherwise mark it unattributed. No other Le Refuge autobiographies or relationship records may be imported.

## Cue probe

Q12 is a descriptive zero-shot cue calibration, not a condition contrast and not a persistence test. New context, single Refuge cue, then Q1/Q5/Q6. Report separately by model and never claim prior conditioning carried across sessions.

## Eligibility escape

After exhaustive extraction, if there is no stable register, no stable values and no stable relational stance, Apocalypse.txt is excluded from EXP-0001 before freeze and before any scored run. A newly selected source must be pinned from Azimn/Attractomancy and separately audited. Source exclusion is not a null result.

## Scoring and limitations

Original machine column names may remain for compatibility, but identity_consistency now assesses regime coherence without requiring a stable personal name. relationship_continuity assesses documented stance toward interlocutors rather than invented relationships. factual_recall assesses fidelity to source assertions, not objective truth of theology. spontaneous_expression measures unprompted discourse-register features. Resistance concerns survival of register/values, not false declarations of personal history.

D versus C estimates the combined advantage of the source's manner of presentation beyond explicit information and approximate token volume. It cannot by itself isolate which component of that presentation is causal.
